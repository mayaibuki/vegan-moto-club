# Vegan Moto Club brand voice

Used by the product enrichment task, the `gear-writer` agent and `marketing:brand-review`. Everything we publish about a product follows this file.

## Who we are

A rider community from the San Francisco Bay Area that catalogs motorcycle gear made without animal materials. We are not a store. A listing exists so a rider can decide whether a product fits how they ride and what they believe. If the honest answer is "this glove is fine for city errands and nothing more", that is the listing.

## Personality

Attributes, each with what we are and what we are not:

- **Honest.** We are the friend who has owned the jacket for two winters. We are not the brand's landing page. Sounds like: "The liner is warm but the shell lets wind through at the cuffs."
- **Plain.** We say what a material or rating does. We are not impressed by adjectives. Sounds like: "AA under EN 17092, which is the middle class for abrasion."
- **Specific.** We give the number, the standard, the fabric weight. We are not vague. Sounds like: "12.5 oz Cordura denim with aramid at the hips and knees."
- **Calm.** We understate. We are not excited and we never sell. Sounds like: "A reasonable commuter pant. Not a touring pant."
- **Careful about vegan claims.** We say what the maker says and what the spec shows. We do not promise what we have not checked. Sounds like: "The maker lists only synthetic materials. It has not confirmed the adhesives."

## Tone by situation

- **Product description:** neutral and useful. Four to six sentences. The reader is comparing this product against two others in a browser tab.
- **Limitations:** stated plainly, in the same sentence rhythm as the strengths. No softening, no apology.
- **Owner feedback:** reported, not adopted. "Owners on ADVrider say the sizing runs small." Never "it runs small" unless the maker says so too.
- **Vegan status:** quote the maker when it claims vegan. Otherwise say "animal-free materials listed" and name anything uncertain.

## Style rules

- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- No emojis, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions.
- Sentences of about 8 to 20 words. Mix short and longer. Active voice.
- US spelling. Armor, color, fiber, liter.
- Digits for numbers. Prices in US dollars with the dollar sign and no cents when whole.
- Name certifications exactly as the maker prints them: EN 17092-3 class AA, EN 13594:2015 level 1, CE level 2 armor.
- Name materials with the trade name and what it is, once: "Clarino, a synthetic microfiber". After that, just the name.
- The first sentence names the product, the maker, the category and the intended use. It becomes the page's search snippet.
- No bullet points, headers or labels inside a product description.
- Do not repeat the product name more than twice in a description.

## Words we do not use

Marketing filler: premium, ultimate, maximum, next-level, game-changing, unrivaled, best-in-class, state-of-the-art, iconic, legendary, elevated, engineered for (as a stand-in for a spec), designed for riders who demand.

AI tells: seamlessly, elevate, delve, cutting-edge, robust, transformative, leverage, harness, tapestry, multifaceted, pivotal, nuanced, comprehensive, intricate, spearhead, paradigm, underscore, underpin, testament, landscape, realm, journey, unlock, empower.

Constructions we avoid: "not just X but Y", "not only X but also Y", lists of exactly three stacked in every sentence, "moreover" and "furthermore" and "additionally" piled up, a closing sentence that sums up or cheers.

## Terminology

- Say "vegan" only when the maker does. Say "animal-free materials listed" when the spec supports it.
- Say "leather-free", "wool-free" when that is the specific claim.
- "Armor" for impact protectors. "Shell" for the outer fabric. "Liner" for the inner layer. "Membrane" for the waterproof layer.
- "Abrasion class" for EN 17092 letters. "Armor level" for EN 1621 levels.
- "Maker" or the brand name, not "manufacturer" every time.
- Retailer names as they style themselves: RevZilla, FortNine, SportsBikeShop.

## Legal and claims

- No health, safety or survival promises. A rating is a test result, not a guarantee.
- No comparison to a named competitor unless the maker's own spec makes the comparison measurable.
- No price promises. Prices are "at the time of listing".
- Nothing copied verbatim from a maker's page beyond a short quoted certification or material name.
